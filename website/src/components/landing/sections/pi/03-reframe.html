worker, trigger, function is the whole interface. small enough for you to hold in your head, small enough for your agent to hold in one context window.

agents aren't a new category here. an agent is a worker. its tools are functions. its memory is state. its orchestration is triggers.

that's the unlock pi can't structurally offer: a tight loop between an agent extending the system and being able to use what it just added. no reload, no rebuild, no restart, no process management.

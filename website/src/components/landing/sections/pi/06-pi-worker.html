already love pi? keep it. iii doesn't ask you to replace your harness, just to stop stopping there.

wrap pi's loop as a worker and the rest of the system, and the rest of your team's workers, become things it can call instead of things it has to reimplement.

what you keep

  • pi's TUI, CLI, RPC and SDK, unchanged
  • your prompts, skills, themes, and extensions
  • the workflow you already have muscle memory for

what you gain

  • triggers, so work continues after the turn ends
  • functions in any language, not just what pi's process can import
  • durable state instead of a session that resets on restart

already have an agent you like? keep it. iii doesn't ask you to replace your coding agent, just to stop it working alone.

wrap your agent's loop as a worker and the rest of the system, and the rest of your team's workers, become things it can call instead of things it has to reimplement.

what you keep

  • the CLI, TUI, or IDE workflow you already have muscle memory for
  • your prompts, skills, themes, and extensions
  • the model and provider you've already chosen

what you gain

  • triggers, so work continues after the turn ends
  • functions in any language, not just what your agent's process can import
  • durable state instead of a session that resets on restart
